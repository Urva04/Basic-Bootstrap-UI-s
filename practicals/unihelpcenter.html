University Admission Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Applicants must have completed higher secondary education (12th grade) with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks. For engineering programs, Mathematics and Physics are mandatory subjects. For business programs, Mathematics or Business Studies is preferred. International students must have equivalent qualifications recognized by the university.

Applications can be submitted online through our official admission portal. The application deadline for the Fall semester is June 30th, and for the Spring semester is November 30th. You will need to create an account, fill in the application form, upload required documents, and pay the application fee of ₹1,500. Early applications are encouraged as seats fill up quickly.

Tuition fees vary by program: Engineering programs cost ₹1,20,000 per semester, Business programs cost ₹90,000 per semester, and Arts & Science programs cost ₹60,000 per semester. Additional fees include examination fees (₹5,000 per year), library fees (₹2,000 per year), and laboratory fees where applicable. Hostel fees are separate and range from ₹40,000 to ₹60,000 per year depending on room type.

Yes, merit-based and need-based scholarships are available for eligible students. Applications must be submitted along with admission forms. Merit scholarships cover up to 100% of tuition fees for students with 90%+ aggregate marks. Need-based scholarships require income proof and cover 25-75% of fees. Special scholarships are also available for sports achievers, cultural talents, and students from underrepresented communities.

Yes, the university provides separate hostel facilities for male and female students within the campus. Rooms are available on twin-sharing and single-occupancy basis. All hostels are equipped with Wi-Fi, mess facilities serving nutritious meals, common rooms with TV, laundry services, and 24/7 security. Hostel allocation is done on a first-come-first-served basis, so apply early to secure a room.

Required documents include: (1) High school transcripts and certificates, (2) Transfer certificate from previous institution, (3) Migration certificate (for out-of-state students), (4) Valid ID proof (Aadhar Card/PAN Card/Passport), (5) Passport-size photographs (6 copies), (6) Caste/category certificate if applicable for reservation, (7) Income proof for need-based scholarships, and (8) Entrance exam score cards (JEE/CAT/University entrance test).
